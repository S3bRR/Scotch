strict native D3D11 and the DXVK version log establish that rendering path.

CGL route. Five repeated cold 64-to-32-bit launch pairs passed after that fix;
the shipping probe also repeats 32-bit DXVK startup.

the core Wine executable remains unchanged, and confirms the repair state
clears. Full prefix snapshot/restore and first adoption of a legacy

The native support archive's hashes, contained symlinks, x86_64 architectures,
code signatures and portable dependencies passed independent verification.

identity probe verifies simultaneous driver instances, AMD/NVIDIA preset driver
versions, and Zink retaining its actual Apple GPU identity.
